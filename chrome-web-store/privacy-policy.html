Nimbus Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Nimbus processes only the URL you choose to shorten and a small local list of recent shortened links.

What Nimbus does

Nimbus is a Chrome extension for creating short links from a URL you paste, the active tab you explicitly open the extension on, or a page/link where you choose the “Shorten with Nimbus” context-menu action.

Data processed

When you ask Nimbus to shorten a URL, that URL is transmitted over HTTPS to the Nimbus URL-shortening backend at nimbus-api-vqsz.onrender.com. The service must process and retain the destination URL and its short-code mapping so the short link can redirect correctly.

The extension also stores up to 20 recent short-link records locally using Chrome storage. Each record can include the short URL, its destination URL, and the time it was created. This local history is used only to show recent links inside the extension and can be cleared from the popup.

Data Nimbus does not intentionally collect

Nimbus does not intentionally collect passwords, payment information, authentication credentials, personal messages, files, precise location, or your general browsing history. Nimbus does not continuously monitor tabs or browsing activity.

Use and sharing

Submitted URLs are used to provide the URL-shortening and redirect service. Nimbus does not sell user data, use submitted URLs for targeted advertising, or use them for behavioral profiling.

Permissions

  • Active tab: reads the current tab URL only when you invoke Nimbus.
  • Storage: keeps recent shortened links in the local Chrome profile.
  • Context menus: adds the “Shorten with Nimbus” right-click action.
  • Notifications: confirms the result of a right-click shortening action.
  • Host access to the Nimbus API: sends shortening requests only to the Nimbus backend.

Retention and deletion

Local recent-link history is limited to 20 records and can be cleared at any time from the extension. Server-side short-link mappings may remain available while the shortening service operates because they are required for redirects.

Security

Nimbus uses HTTPS for communication with its hosted backend. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, but access is limited to what is necessary to provide the shortening feature.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same public policy URL with a revised “Last updated” date.
