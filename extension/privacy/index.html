Nimbus Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

Nimbus is a Chrome extension that helps you create shorter URLs from your current browser tab or from a URL you enter manually.

Information Nimbus processes

When you shorten a URL, Nimbus sends that URL to the Nimbus URL-shortening service so that a shortened URL can be created.

The URL submitted for shortening may be stored by the Nimbus service as part of the shortened-link mapping required to redirect the shortened URL to its destination.

Nimbus also stores a limited history of your recently shortened links locally in your browser using Chrome's storage API. This local history is not sent to Nimbus solely for the purpose of displaying your local history.

Information Nimbus does not intentionally collect

Nimbus does not intentionally collect your general browsing history, passwords, payment information, or authentication credentials.

How information is used

Submitted URLs are used to create and operate shortened links. Local browser history is used to display your recently created shortened links inside the extension.

Sharing

Nimbus does not sell your information. We do not use submitted URLs for advertising or behavioral profiling.

Data retention

The extension keeps up to 20 recent shortened links in local Chrome storage. You can remove this local history at any time using the Clear button in the extension.

Server-side shortened URL records may remain available for the operation of the shortening service.

Third-party services

Nimbus uses its hosted URL-shortening backend to process shortening requests. Your submitted URL must be transmitted to that service for Nimbus to create a shortened URL.

Security

Nimbus takes reasonable measures to protect the information processed by the service. However, no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will be published on this page.
