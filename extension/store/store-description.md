# Nimbus URL Shortener

Shorten long URLs instantly from your Chrome browser.

Nimbus gives you a fast, simple way to create short links without opening a separate website.

## Why Nimbus?

**One-click shortening**
Use the current tab's URL automatically or paste any HTTP/HTTPS URL.

**Fast and simple**
Create a short link directly from the Chrome toolbar.

**Right-click support**
Shorten a page or link from Chrome's context menu with "Shorten with Nimbus."

**Copy instantly**
Copy your new short URL with one click.

**Recent links**
Nimbus keeps a small local history of your recently shortened links so you can quickly find and copy them again.

## How it works

1. Open Nimbus from the Chrome toolbar.
2. Nimbus loads the current tab's URL automatically.
3. Click **Shorten URL**.
4. Copy your new short link.

You can also right-click a page or link and choose **Shorten with Nimbus**.

## Privacy

Nimbus sends URLs you choose to shorten to the Nimbus URL-shortening service so a short link can be created. Recent-link history is stored locally in your browser.

Nimbus does not sell your information or use submitted URLs for advertising or behavioral profiling.

## Permissions

Nimbus uses:

* **Active Tab** to read the current page URL when you choose to shorten it.
* **Storage** to save your Nimbus settings and local recent-link history.
* **Context Menus** to provide right-click URL shortening.

Nimbus is designed to do one thing well: turn long links into short, shareable URLs.
